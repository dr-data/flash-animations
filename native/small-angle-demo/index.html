What should you notice?

If the physical diameter stays fixed, moving the object farther away makes its angular size smaller.

If the distance stays fixed, increasing the diameter makes its angular size larger.

Current ratio 2 ÷ 40 = 0.0500 The approximation is most accurate when this ratio is small.
